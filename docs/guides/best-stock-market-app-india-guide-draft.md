## Intro

Picking a stock market app means picking two things at once: a broker that executes your trades, and (ideally) an information source that keeps you ahead of market-moving news. Most "best app" lists only cover the first. This guide does both: ten brokers compared honestly on brokerage, features and limitations — then the one information gap every trading app shares, and how to cover it.

## H2: What "best" means for a stock market app

Five things actually matter: **brokerage** (what each trade costs you), **uptime during market hours** (apps that freeze at 9:15 are a special kind of pain), **charting and order types** for active traders, **research quality** for investors, and **ease of KYC** for first-timers. Everything else is marketing.

## H2: The 10 best stock market apps in India (2026)

### H3: Zerodha (Kite) — best for low-cost, stable trading

India's best-known discount broker. Equity delivery is genuinely free for retail individuals, intraday and F&O cost 0.03% or ₹20 per order (whichever is lower; options flat ₹20). Kite was the first retail platform in India with 20-level market depth, and its stability during volatile sessions is the industry benchmark. Limitation: demat AMC is ₹300/year + GST, and there's no in-house research desk.

### H3: Groww — best for beginners

The simplest onboarding in the industry and a clean app that first-timers actually understand. Note: delivery is NOT free anymore — it's ₹20 or 0.1% per order with a ₹5 minimum (free delivery ended mid-2024). For pros, "915 by Groww" is a proper pro terminal with customisable dashboards, straddle charts and an option chain showing Greeks and open interest. Limitation: trades funded from UPI Mandate balance cost 1% of order value with no cap.

### H3: Upstox — best for fast execution / intraday

Built for speed: free API access, up to 5x intraday margin on equity, and a Plus VIP tier for heavy traders. Brokerage is ₹20 per order on delivery (no free delivery here) and ₹20 or 0.1% (lower) on intraday; futures ₹20 or 0.05% (lower), options flat ₹20. Limitation: delivery at ₹20/order stings versus Zerodha's ₹0.

### H3: Angel One — best for advisory + research blend

The full-service broker that went discount: free research reports, portfolio advisory and analyser tools, plus robo-advisory — rare at this price. Delivery is ₹20 or 0.1% (lower, ₹5 minimum); intraday is ₹20 or 0.1% (lower) after a hike from 0.03% in November 2025 — old guides quoting 0.03% are outdated. F&O flat ₹20/order. Limitation: margin funding (MTF) interest runs 14.99% p.a.

### H3: 5paisa — best for budget trading

Dead simple pricing: flat ₹20 per order across every segment — delivery, intraday, futures and options. A SEBI-registered research desk and FnO 360 (16+ real-time Greeks) sweeten the deal for derivatives traders. Limitation: ₹20 flat even on delivery means small-ticket investors pay ₹20 + ₹20 on buy and sell regardless of trade size.

### H3: ICICI Direct — best for banking-integrated investors

The 3-in-1 account (bank + trading + demat) that made online investing mainstream. Pricing is plan-based: the iValue plan gives flat ₹20/order on intraday and F&O; delivery stays percentage-based (default ~0.29%, down to 0.07% lifetime on the ₹9,999 Prime plan). Free research and advisory included. Limitation: delivery never goes flat-₹20, and demat AMC is ₹700/year (₹300 with iValue).

### H3: HDFC Sky — best for brand trust + stability

HDFC Securities' discount arm, open to everyone (no HDFC Bank account needed). Flat ₹20 or 0.1% (lower) across delivery, intraday and F&O; zero brokerage for 18–25-year-olds and on ETFs. TradingView charts, SKY Signals and even 10,000+ US stocks. Limitation: research depth trails the flagship HDFC Securities, and delivery still costs ₹20/order.

### H3: Kotak Securities — best for trusted 3-in-1 investing

The Trinity 3-in-1 account links Kotak Bank savings, trading and demat with automatic fund settlement — the smoothest money movement of any broker here. "Trade Free" gives ₹0 brokerage for the first 30 days only; after that delivery is 0.20%, intraday ₹10 or 0.05% (lower), F&O ₹10/order. Limitation: the zero window is strictly 30 days — read the plan before assuming it's free forever.

### H3: Motilal Oswal — best for deep research investors

If research is your religion, this is the temple: an award-winning analyst desk with deep sector coverage. Brokerage is old-school percentage: 0.20% delivery, 0.02% intraday, futures 0.02%, options ₹20/lot. Limitation: 0.20% delivery is uncapped and expensive for large orders; margin funding interest ~19.3% p.a.

### H3: Shoonya — best for zero-brokerage delivery

The only broker here with genuinely ₹0 equity delivery brokerage (confirmed on its official FAQ). Intraday and F&O cost 0.03% or ₹5 per order (lower) — the all-segment zero model ended in December 2024, so don't believe old "everything free" claims. Its SensAI tool analyses news sentiment on stocks with AI. Limitation: smaller support footprint than the big brokers.

To be clear: **BSE Nexus is not a broker or trading app**, and we don't rank it as one. You can't place trades here. It belongs on this list because it covers the one thing every app above structurally misses — **live market-moving information**. Brokers execute your orders; exchange filings move the prices. Results, board meetings, block deals and clarifications hit BSE as announcements *first*.

BSE Nexus is a real-time BSE announcements feed: every filing as it lands on the exchange, a board-meeting and results calendar, and instant alerts on your watchlist companies. The filings feed and results calendar are free; Pro (from ₹59/week) adds unlimited watchlists, instant Telegram filing alerts, and AI filing summaries — with a 1-week free Pro trial on sign-in, no card required.

## H2: Which app should you pick?

- **Beginner investor:** Groww for the simplest start; Zerodha if you want the lowest long-term cost (free delivery).
- **Active / intraday trader:** Upstox or Shoonya for execution cost; Zerodha for platform stability at 9:15.
- **Long-term / research-driven investor:** Motilal Oswal or ICICI Direct for research; Angel One for advisory at discount prices.
- **Everyone:** whichever broker you pick, keep a real-time filings feed open alongside it. Your broker executes the trade; the filing tells you why the price moved.

## H2: The one thing no trading app gives you

Every app above is built for execution — placing orders, charting, tracking your portfolio. None of them gives you a real-time, exchange-wide push feed of BSE filings. The closest is ICICI Direct's Smart Stock Alerts, which cover corporate announcements only for stocks you already hold, via mailer/SMS/WhatsApp. Everything else is generic market news you pull manually.

But prices move on filings: a surprise board meeting, an auditor resignation, a block deal, a regulatory clarification. Those land on the exchange first — hours before they reach any broker app's news section. That's the gap BSE Nexus fills: every BSE announcement as it lands, with instant alerts on your watchlist companies, so the news never ambushes your position.

## H2: Frequently asked questions

**Q1. Which is the best stock market app in India for beginners?**
Groww for the easiest onboarding, or Zerodha for the lowest long-term cost with free equity delivery. Both have clean mobile apps and simple KYC. Start with one; switching brokers later is easy since your demat account stays yours.

**Q2. Which app has the lowest brokerage in India?**
For delivery, Zerodha and Shoonya charge ₹0. For intraday and F&O, most discount brokers cluster at flat ₹20/order (Shoonya at ₹5). Full-service brokers like ICICI Direct and Motilal Oswal charge percentage-based brokerage, which costs more on large orders.

**Q3. Zerodha vs Groww — which is better?**
Zerodha wins on cost (free delivery, stable Kite platform); Groww wins on simplicity and its 915 pro terminal for advanced traders. Note Groww's delivery is no longer free (₹5 minimum per order). Pick Zerodha to minimise cost, Groww for the easiest experience.

**Q4. Are trading apps safe? Is my money protected?**
Yes, with the standard protections: your securities sit in a demat account with NSDL or CDSL — not with your broker — so they remain safe even if the broker shuts down. SEBI mandates segregation of client funds and securities, and exchanges maintain an Investor Protection Fund compensating up to ₹25 lakh per client claim if a broker defaults. Always verify a broker's SEBI registration before opening an account.

**Q5. Can I invest in IPOs and mutual funds from these apps?**
Mostly yes. Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Angel One and 5paisa all support IPO applications (UPI-based) and mutual fund investing. Full-service brokers add bonds, NCDs and more. Check the specific app's product list — mutual fund support is near-universal now.

**Q6. Which app is best for intraday trading?**
Upstox and Shoonya for low execution cost and fast platforms; Zerodha's Kite for the most battle-tested stability during volatile opens. Intraday is also where brokerage matters most — flat ₹20/order beats percentage plans once your trade size grows.

**Q7. Is there an app that shows live BSE company announcements?**
Broker apps show generic market news, not live filings. ICICI Direct alerts you about corporate announcements only for stocks you hold. For a real-time, exchange-wide BSE announcements feed with instant push alerts, use a dedicated filings app like BSE Nexus alongside your broker.
