## H2: What a stock screener actually does (and doesn't do)

A screener is a filtering tool, not a recommendation engine. You set conditions — say, market cap above ₹1,000 crore, debt-to-equity below 0.5, and 5-year profit growth above 15% — and it returns every stock matching them. What it doesn't do:

- **It doesn't recommend stocks.** The output is a list, not advice.
- **It doesn't find guaranteed multibaggers.** Backtested screens look brilliant in hindsight; future returns are never guaranteed by any filter.
- **It doesn't replace research.** A screener tells you *where to look*, not *what to buy*. Reading the actual financials and filings still matters.

### H3: Screener.in — best for fundamental deep-dives

The default choice for serious fundamental investors. A custom formula-based query language, 10+ years of annual financial data with full quarterly statements, and coverage of 5,000+ NSE/BSE stocks. Free for individual investors, which is why it tops nearly every comparison list. Its own feature list also includes a latest-announcements section with alert options — handy, though it's a pull-style company page rather than an instant exchange-wide feed.

### H3: Tickertape — best for clean, visual research

If Screener.in feels like a spreadsheet, Tickertape feels like a product. 200+ filters, scorecards, forecasts and peer comparison in a clean interface that beginners actually enjoy using. Free basic tier; premium at ₹249/month, ₹699 for 3 months, or ₹2,399/year.

### H3: ChartInk — best free technical scanner

The go-to free tool for technical traders. Build custom scanners from indicators (RSI, moving averages, volume spikes) with an intuitive no-code interface, running on live market data. Most features are free; real-time alerts via SMS/email/web sit on paid plans. It also ships a library of readymade screeners you can run as-is.

### H3: Trendlyne — best for DVM scores & consensus

Trendlyne's signature is the DVM framework — Durability, Valuation and Momentum scores that compress a company's quality into three numbers. ### H3: ScanX by Dhan — best for intraday momentum

## H2: What screeners miss (and how to cover it)

Every screener in this list runs on processed financial data — quarterly results, ratios, price history. That data has lag built in. But the events that move prices *today* — a surprise board meeting, an auditor resignation, a block deal, a regulatory clarification — arrive as exchange filings first, hours or days before they show up in any screener's dataset.

Some tools gesture at this: Screener.in and Finology Ticker both have latest-announcements sections on company pages (Screener.in even supports announcement alerts). But those are pull-style pages you check manually — not an instant, push-alert feed of every filing hitting the exchange.

That's the gap BSE Nexus fills: a real-time BSE announcements feed with instant alerts on your watchlist companies, so the filing never blindsides the screen. Use any screener above to *find* the stock; use a filings feed to *stay ahead of its news*.

## H2: Frequently asked questions

**Q1. Which is the best stock screener in India?**
There isn't one winner — it depends on your style. Screener.in leads for fundamental deep-dives, ChartInk for free technical scans, Tickertape for clean visual research, Trendlyne for DVM scores, ScanX for intraday momentum on Dhan, and Finology Ticker for ready-made screens. All have usable free tiers.

**Q2. Is Screener.in really free? What are its limits?**
Yes — Screener.in is free for individual investors, with 10+ years of financial data, quarterly statements and a custom query language included. Premium tiers exist for heavier users; check screener.in for current pricing.

**Q3. Fundamental vs technical screener — which do I need?**
Long-term investors need fundamental screeners (financial ratios, growth, valuation); short-term traders need technical scanners (price action, indicators, volume). Many investors eventually use both — for example, Screener.in for the shortlist and ChartInk for timing entries.

**Q4. Can a screener find multibagger stocks?**
No screener can guarantee multibaggers. Screens like "high ROCE, low debt" surface quality companies, but future returns depend on business execution, valuation at purchase and market conditions — none of which a filter can predict.

**Q5. What filters should beginners start with?**
Keep it simple: market cap above ₹1,000 crore (avoids illiquid microcaps), debt-to-equity below 1, positive profit growth over 3–5 years, and ROCE above 15%. Four filters, quality-leaning, easy to understand.

**Q6. Do screeners show real-time data?**
Price data on most screeners is live or near-live during market hours, but fundamental data updates only when results are processed — always with a lag. Corporate events hit the exchange as filings first, before any screener reflects them.

**Q7. Is there a screener that also shows live company news and filings?**
Not really. Screener.in and Finology Ticker have latest-announcements sections on company pages, but they're pull-style pages, not instant push feeds. For real-time BSE filings with instant alerts, pair your screener with a dedicated announcements feed like BSE Nexus.
