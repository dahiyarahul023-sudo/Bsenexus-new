# Source-linked Radar reaction journal and morning watch

- Upstream main: 0235e85adf411f1ce7b8e1b0442c465706da1628. User authorizes implementation, operational database additions, and automatic merge after passing checks.
- Goal: retain the specific news/ruling detail, learn descriptive historical reaction patterns over 30/60/365 days, distinguish observed volatility from unopened/unfinished sessions, and surface fresh pending news around the next trading open.
- Existing source: immutable Radar updates, exact listed-company identity, and private immutable official NSE/BSE daily snapshots. Existing boot/watchdog/secret-authenticated cron captures prices. The feed is capped at 200 events; historical learning therefore scans the updates table independently using a durable cursor, with recent and pending retry queues.
- Changed consumers: Radar API list and individual detail, typed feed state, preview/company cards, modal reaction filters/detail, existing background price worker. Ordinary news, Telegram delivery/recipients, follows, auth, subscription policy, payments, result classification and original evidence remain compatible. No buy/sell recommendation, target or causal finding is generated.
- Journal: one source-update/security entry, original publication/source/excerpt, fact type, stated monetary amount, explicit interim/final scope, authority, expected first full session, assessment clock, sourced price and optional historical baseline. Service-only invoker RPC binds the source, company and exact stored official price. Duplicate writes are safe and observed evidence cannot be erased by later pending/failed retries. RLS and revoked browser privileges match existing Radar tables.
- Learning: rolling 30/60/365-day descriptive aggregates group fact/scope/source type. One company/session sample per group; overlapping distinct proceedings are excluded from statistics and retained in the journal. Missing prices are excluded rather than treated as zero movement. Early samples (<20) remain explicitly limited. This accumulates empirical statistics; it does not retrain a language model or establish the cause of a move.
- Initial rule: absolute final close change >=3% OR high-low range >=5% of previous close. Optional unusual-move comparison requires ten pre-publication sessions and >=2x median absolute close movement (minimum 3%). Daily final reports do not reveal the exact intraday reaction or market/sector-adjusted abnormal returns. Corporate actions and concurrent news remain confounders.
- Timing: pre-open news uses that full session; intraday/after-close news uses the next full session so overlapping pre-publication trading cannot become evidence. Morning window is 90 minutes before through 45 minutes after the verified open. Records remain pending until final daily data is available; elapsed missing sessions say Awaiting price data. News older than the first recorded assessment is not presented as freshly observed.
- Calendar: explicit 2026 holidays from https://www.nseindia.com/resources/exchange-communication-holidays and regular hours from https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/market-timings. Special 8 November 2026 session: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR76941.pdf. Fixed NSE holiday-master endpoint refreshes a validated current-year calendar; failure retries hourly. Unknown years remain calendar-pending, with no invented next-open claim. No yearless lunar-holiday reuse. Exceptional future sessions require their own verified timings.
- Jobs: existing hosting scheduler remains the owner; no browser visit required. New worker coalesces per process, bounds reads/writes and concurrency, retries failed storage after one minute and checkpoints completed historical scanning before later work. Source rows/prices and the durable cursor survive restarts. Historical rows without saved prices remain awaiting-data; no year-long exchange download is initiated. Data accumulates from available sources; this is not a claim of existing one-year coverage.
- UI: compact badges, All reactions / Volatility observed / Awaiting session / Morning watch filters; morning prioritization in the two-card preview. Source wording/amount/scope, actual observed numbers, dates, sample sizes and collapsed method are in the existing dialog. Saved research survives refresh failure. Missing research does not hide news. Account follows, scope, keyboard/modal ownership, themes and scroll remain shared.
- Verification: focused pure/worker/SQL/API/SSR tests; isolated PGlite source/price binding, RLS/denied roles, duplicate/retry preservation, independent sample counting and overlapping proceedings; full tests/typecheck/build/maps; actual browser fixture for morning priority, filters, sourced detail, retained outage, modal dismissal and 360/390/768/1440px light/dark. Live iPhone, intraday prices and deployment are separate evidence.

Production preparation and CI/merge results are reported on the pull request. No historical news/price/follow rows are rewritten.
